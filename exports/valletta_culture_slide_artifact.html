Culture Framework
Physical Security Division

Physical Security
Force Culture

The behaviors and mindsets that define how every officer shows up, every shift.

Core Purpose

Protect people. Secure assets. Enable operations.

Every action, decision, and interaction supports the safety, security, and success of the organization.

01

You Matter

PEOPLE FIRST CULTURE
  • Treat everyone with dignity and respect.
  • Prioritize safety and wellbeing.
  • Look out for one another.
  • Develop and mentor teammates.
  • Recognize contributions and successes.

Every person deserves to go home safe and know they are valued.

Mindset
02

Professional and Consistent

TRUSTED THROUGH EXCELLENCE
  • Follow standards and procedures.
  • Maintain appearance and readiness.
  • Demonstrate discipline and accountability.
  • Treat every situation objectively.
  • Deliver the same level of service every shift, every day.

Our reputation is earned through consistency.

Mindset
03

Collaborate and Win

ONE TEAM, ONE MISSION
  • Share information proactively.
  • Support fellow officers and business partners.
  • Build relationships across departments.
  • Seek solutions, not blame.
  • Celebrate team achievements.

We are stronger together than we are individually.

Mindset
04

Say Something

SEE IT. OWN IT. REPORT IT.
  • Report concerns immediately.
  • Escalate issues without hesitation.
  • Challenge unsafe conditions respectfully.
  • Encourage open communication.
  • Learn from incidents and near misses.

Silence creates risk. Speaking up creates safety.

Mindset
05

Action Oriented

DECISIVE WHEN IT MATTERS
  • Respond promptly.
  • Take initiative.
  • Solve problems at the lowest level possible.
  • Adapt to changing circumstances.
  • Drive issues to resolution.

When action is required, we lead.

Mindset
Vigilance • Integrity • Respect • Accountability • Service

Protecting Today. Enabling Tomorrow.
