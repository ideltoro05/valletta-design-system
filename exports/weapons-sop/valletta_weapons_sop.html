SOP – Firearms Loading and Unloading
1. Purpose
1.1To establish safe, standardized, and accountable procedures for arming and disarming personnel, loading and unloading firearms, managing ammunition, and maintaining armory security during all guard force operations
2. Scope
2.1This SOP applies to all armed security personnel, supervisors, armorers, and other authorized personnel who issue, receive, handle, store, transport, or account for firearms and ammunition within the armory.
3. Definitions and Acronyms
3.1BCD – Bullet Containment Device
3.2Armory – Secure location used for storage and accountability of firearms and ammunition.
3.3Facility Ready – Condition of in-service duty firearms
3.3.1Rifle – Full magazine inserted with empty chamber unless otherwise authorized.
3.3.2Pistol – Full magazine inserted with a round chambered.
3.4Safe Firearm – Firearm verified clear of ammunition and configured for storage.
4. Responsibility and Authority
4.1Supervisors
4.1.1Control access to the armory and firearms safe.
4.1.2Open and secure safes at shift change
4.1.3Observe and direct all loading and unloading operations
4.1.4Verify compliance with this SOP and stop unsafe acts immediately
4.1.5Conduct required daily armory inventory, records and discrepancy reporting
4.2Armed Officers
4.2.1Follow all firearm safety rules
4.2.2Comply with Supervisor commands during loading/unloading
4.2.3Immediately report unsafe conditions, damaged equipment, or ammunition discrepancies.
4.3Site Trainers
4.3.1Maintain serviceability of firearms and ammunition.
4.3.2Ensure inspections, maintenance, storage, and records are current.
5. General Firearms Safety Rules
5.1Treat every firearm as if it is loaded.
5.2Keep muzzle pointed in a safe direction at all times.
5.3Keep fingers off the trigger, outside the trigger guard, and safety engaged until you decide to fire.
5.4Be sure of your target and what’s beyond it.

6. Supervisor Requirements During Shift Change
6.1Supervisor Shall:
6.1.1Open the firearm safe and maintain positive control of issued firearms.
6.1.2Observe every loading and unloading action from start to finish.
6.1.3Verify firearms are clear before issue and before storage.
6.1.4Ensure use of the BCD during all chambering and clearing operations.
6.1.5Verify ammunition counts, magazine counts, and firearm serial numbers as required.
6.1.6Halt any unsafe act immediately.
6.1.7Ensure all issued firearms, ammunition, and equipment are accounted for before personnel depart.
6.1.8Secure all safes and armory access points after completion of shift-change operations.
6.1.9Maintain constant visual and physical control of contents inside the safe.
7. Firearm Loading Procedure
7.1Supervisor will retrieve the correct firearm from storage.
7.2Insert muzzle into the BCD, visually verifying serial number.
7.2.1Rifle – Verify safety is engaged.
7.3Lock the action to the rear, visually and physically verifying the firearm is clear.
7.4The supervisor will transfer control of the firearm to the officer.
7.5While still pointed into the BCD, the officer will re-verify the firearm is clear.
7.6Source of Feed:
7.6.1Pistol – Insert magazine and release the action, chambering a round.
7.6.2Rifle – Release the action before inserting magazine. No round will be chambered.
7.7Retain the firearm.
NOTE
Officer may conduct press checks or magazine retention tests.

8. Firearm Unloading Procedure
8.1Insert muzzle into the BCD, visually verifying serial number.
8.1.1Rifle – Verify safety is engaged.
8.2Remove the magazine.
8.3Lock action to the rear, ejecting the chambered round, if present.
8.4Visually and physically verify the firearm is clear.
8.5The officer will transfer control of the firearm to the supervisor.
8.6While still pointed into the BCD, the supervisor will re-verify the firearm is clear.
8.6.1Rifle – Verify safety is engaged.
8.7Release the action and place the firearm into storage.
8.8Collect and replace ejected round if present, return all magazines to the proper storage.
9. Firearms Storage Requirements
9.1Firearms shall be stored unloaded unless operation requirements dictate otherwise
9.2Out-of-service firearms shall be tagged or segregated from service firearms.
9.3Storage containers/firearms safes shall remain locked when not under direct supervisory observation and control.
10. Ammunition Accountability and Management
10.1Only authorized duty ammunition shall be issued.
10.2Ammunition counts shall be verified during issue and turn-in.
10.3Damaged or suspected ammunition shall be removed.
10.4Duty ammunition shall be inspected and rotated in accordance with organizational policy
10.5Monthly ammunition inventories shall be completed, reconciled, and documented by the Site Trainer. Any discrepancies shall be immediately reported to the National Training Manager and Site Manager and investigated in accordance with established procedures.
11. Documentation and Reporting
11.1All firearms issues, returns, inventories, discrepancies, damaged ammunition, unsafe acts, training completion, SOP acknowledgments, and out-of-service firearms shall be documented by the supervisor or trainer in the records management systems.
11.2Training records and employee acknowledgments for this SOP shall be maintained by Training management and retained in accordance with company record retention requirements.
11.3Training management shall ensure personnel under their supervision have completed all required training and acknowledgments for this SOP.
11.4Supervisors shall promptly document and report any violation of this SOP through established reporting channels.

12. Training and Compliance
12.1Personnel shall receive initial training on this SOP prior to being issued a firearm, assigned to an armed post, or performing any duties covered by this SOP. Refresher training shall be conducted periodically as determined by organizational requirements.
12.2Upon completion of training, personnel shall acknowledge in writing or through an approved electronic system that they have received, reviewed, understand, and will comply with this SOP. Documentation of training and acknowledgment shall be maintained in accordance with Section 12.2.
12.3Supervisors or Trainers shall conduct observations, inspections, and corrective actions to ensure continued compliance with this SOP.
12.4Failure to comply with the requirements of this SOP may result in corrective action up to and including removal from armed duties, suspension of firearm authorization, disciplinary action, or other corrective measures in accordance with established Employee Relations policies and procedures.
12.5Personnel who have not completed the required training and acknowledgment process shall not be issued a firearm, ammunition, or assigned to an armed post until all requirements have been satisfied and documented.
